# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - Minecraft 26.1.2

First release.

- Infinity Cell: imprint one item and the ME network can extract it without limit
- Infinity Fluid Cell: the same for one fluid
- Infinity Energy Cell (with Applied Flux): charge it once and it supplies unlimited FE
- Material chain from AE2's singularity: Compressed Singularity, Infinity Core, blank cells
- Singularity Compressor and Infinity Imprinter machines, both accepting Acceleration Cards
- JEI recipe category for the Singularity Compressor
- Guide pages in the AE2 guide (English and Japanese)
- Item and fluid tags `applied_everything:imprint_blacklist` for modpack makers
